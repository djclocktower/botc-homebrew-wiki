# Character ideas, ranked by how unlike the wiki they are

Measured against 3403 characters (every published page on botchomebrew.wiki plus the official roster). **Novelty** is the idea's average distance to its 5 nearest characters, in units of the typical spacing between existing characters (1.0 = an ordinary character; higher = further from anything that exists). **Beats** is the share of existing characters that sit closer to their own neighbours than this idea does.

Generated by `migration/character-ideas/novelty.py`; see its header for the method.

## 1. Interregnum (Loric)

**Novelty 1.49** - beats 99.4% of existing characters

> [No Demon, +1 Minion] Each night*, the Storyteller chooses a player to die. Each dawn, the number of alive Minions is announced. Good wins when no Minions live.

*"The throne below is empty. Its servants squabble in the dark, and the dark kills whoever it likes."*

**Tags:** Setup, Nonconformist, Death, Public, Information

A Demonless game where the Storyteller does the killing and the town hunts Minions against a public body count.

Nearest existing: [Fascist](https://botchomebrew.wiki/c/imppreposterous-syncretastrophy/fascist) by christoph-ehm (1.38); [Plotter (Role)](https://botchomebrew.wiki/c/imppreposterous-syncretastrophy/plotter-role) by christoph-ehm (1.46); [Nightmare](https://botchomebrew.wiki/c/imppreposterous-syncretastrophy/nightmare) by christoph-ehm (1.46)
