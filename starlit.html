Starlit Gallery

Overview

Created by @Harry & Co and @Bendan07, the creators of Travel by the Starlight, the Starlit Gallery contains 100 unique, hand-drawn, and black-and-white icons, 5 texture variants, and handful of icon templates, a custom script logo, and a blank character token image for you to use on your botc homebrew characters.

Notably, you do not need to credit us when you use* our resources - however, a shout-out would be appreciated, since it helps promote the collection.
*Please refrain from using our work for commercial products, though!

So take a look, visitors, and experience the wonders of craft, of creativity, of human expression.
