Who Am I?

The daily homebrew puzzle. A new character every day at midnight UTC — guess it from its ability.

Shuffling the bag…

How to play
  • Every day the wiki picks one homebrew character. Everybody gets the same one.
  • You see its ability with its own name blacked out. Type a character's name and pick it from the list to guess.
  • Each guess is compared with the answer: the same, close (the same alignment, or some tags in common), different.
  • After two wrong guesses you see the icon's silhouette; after four, the name's length and first letter.
  • You have six guesses. Your streak is kept in this browser only.
