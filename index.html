About This Wiki

The BOTC HomeBrew Wiki is a fan wiki of homebrew characters, scripts and collections for Blood on the Clocktower. Character pages have almanac entries and export to the official script tool.

Anyone can add a character with the character editor. Every page credits the character’s original creator.

This website was created by Dj_Dj_Dj, with the assistance of Claude Code, and is hosted on Cloudflare.

Fan-made content. Not affiliated with The Pandemonium Institute. For the official game, rules, and merchandise, visit bloodontheclocktower.com.
