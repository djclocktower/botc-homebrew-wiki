Import from Bloodstar

Paste a link to a Bloodstar project and the whole thing comes across: every character with its art, its flavour text, its overview, its examples, its how-to-run and its tips, plus the jinxes, the night order, and the synopsis and changelog as a script or collection page here. Both of the project's published files are read — the script.json for the mechanics and the almanac.html for everything a reader actually comes to an almanac for.

Where it goes

The pages themselves

Drafts are yours alone until you publish them, which is worth having for an import this size.

Official characters

Scripts carry official characters alongside homebrew ones, and Bloodstar exports them as ordinary entries. This wiki is for homebrew, so an official character never gets a page here — it already has one on the official wiki, and its art is not ours to host. The script keeps it: the roster points at the official character and its name on the page links straight through. That is not a setting.

A reworked character is a different character wearing a familiar name — pointing at the official one would put an ability on the script that the author did not write.

What the almanac becomes

Every line of this is what a character page here ends up saying. The defaults line each Bloodstar section up with the wiki section that does the same job.

Art

The project's own pages

The synopsis, the overview and the changelog are pages in the Bloodstar almanac. Here they belong on the script or collection page.

A changelog is long and text-first, which is what wiki pages are for. It is linked from the script page's Pages section.

Everything else

A jinx on a character travels with it to every script that holds both ends. A jinx between two official characters can only ever be a script rule, and is stored that way whatever this says.
Who can edit these pages afterwards
Applied to every page this import makes. You can change it per page afterwards.

Tags

Bloodstar has no tags and this wiki leans on them — a character with none reads as Partial and is hidden from the browse pages until someone adds one. Pick the characters a tag is true of, then give it to all of them at once.
