# Blood on the Clocktower decorations

Collected 2026-10-24 from botc.app, script.bloodontheclocktower.com and
bloodontheclocktower.com. All artwork belongs to The Pandemonium Institute. Fancy Scripts
offers them as art choices and stickers: ART_CHOICES and DECOR_STICKERS in ../script.js
are the list. The six images over 1 MB have a .webp copy at 1600px beside them, and the
copy is what the tool loads.

```

skulls/cut-from-logos/   The edition skulls with the title lettering cut out
                         of the official logos. The Tomb has a gap at the jaw
                         where the word "The" was painted over it.
                         Trouble Brewing, Bad Moon Rising and Travellers &
                         Fabled come from small logos (under 600px).
skulls/botc-app/         The 200px edition pictures botc.app shows beside a
                         script (tb, bmr, snv skulls; custom lamp; homebrew crate).

botc-app/<edition>/      Each edition's token decorations from botc.app:
                         leaf-left/right/top*/orange   leaves and the orange flower
                         firstnight / othernight       the night-order leaves
                         sheet-bottom                  the garland under a character sheet
                         token                         the blank token disc
                         The leaf files sit on a token-sized canvas, in the spot
                         they take on a token. trimmed/ holds the same leaves cut
                         down to just the picture.
botc-app/ornaments/      butterfly, clouds, separators, shroud banner.

website/                 Cutouts from the official shop site: leaf branches and
                         sprigs, monstera leaf, feather, books and candle, barrel,
                         raven on skull, crystal rock, laurels, clocktower,
                         difficulty badges. Full original size.

script-tool/             Leaf sprigs and the line break from the official
                         script tool.
```
