Mass Upload Characters

Upload a whole script JSON or a master list of characters (official schema) and create a wiki page for every character in it — all at once. Characters need at least a name, team and ability. If a character's image is an array, the second entry is saved as its alternate art.

Importing a Bloodstar project? Bloodstar Import reads its almanac as well as its JSON, so the pages arrive with their flavour text, overviews, examples, how-to-run and tips already written.
