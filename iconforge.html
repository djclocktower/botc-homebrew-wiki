The forge

Artwork

0px is razor sharp. The outer edge shapes the silhouette; the inner edge shapes the white detail lines inside it.

Background

Works out where your subject is and cuts everything else away — photos, coloured paper, messy scans, anything the two keys below cannot handle. The first run downloads about 45 MB and can take a minute; your browser keeps it for next time. It works away in the background, so the page stays usable and you can stop it whenever you like.

Lower keeps faint lines and small detached parts; higher strips more leftover smudges.

Instant brightness key — best for clean black line art on white paper.

Pick the background colour straight from your artwork — anything near it becomes transparent. Works for black, white, green screen or coloured paper.

Detail & depth

White swoosh strokes along the lit edges of the shape — the hand-drawn look, worked out from the silhouette itself.

Lights the silhouette like a rounded shape — depth for completely flat art. Follows the highlight angle.

Finds anything much darker or brighter than its surroundings — handle wraps, stitching, hatching — and draws it as a white detail line. Works in Silhouette, White key and Smart remove modes.

Draws a thin white line where neighbouring colours differ — handle against cord, sleeve against skin — even when they are equally bright.

Character type

Good texture left of the white divider, evil right of it — the official Traveller look.

Paint bucket

Fill enclosed spaces by hand: pick Colour or Parchment, then tap a space in the preview. Unlike “Fill enclosed spaces”, you choose each region and its fill. Hatch adds shading lines to whatever you tap instead.

    Hatching

    Hand-drawn shading lines, like the ink hatching on the official icons. With no regions picked in the paint bucket, the whole colour body is hatched.

    Texture grade

    Colour texture

    Vibrance lifts dull tones without blowing out vivid ones; gradient darkens toward the bottom for extra weight.

    Parchment texture

    Grades the parchment used for detail regions, and for the border when it is set to cream.

    Texture placement

    Official icons are each a random crop of one big texture sheet — pan, zoom and spin until the grain sits right. The dice take a fresh crop.

    Border & shadow

    A soft shadow behind the whole sticker. Wiki icons are usually flat, so leave it low unless you are making a preview image.

    Orientation

    Applied to the finished icon. It shrinks a little when rotated so the corners never get cropped.

    Download

    Getting a good icon

    • Bold, closed shapes read best. The forge decides what is body and what is detail — thin grey pencil lines give it nothing to hold onto.
    • Line art on white paper? Set Background to White key. A photo, or coloured paper? Use Smart remove, which cuts the subject out for you.
    • Something filled that should be hollow (or the other way round)? Turn off Fill enclosed spaces, then use the paint bucket to choose each region yourself.
    • Every icon is a different crop of the same texture sheet — roll the dice under Texture placement so yours does not look identical to the last one.
    • Save at 591 px for the wiki, then add it in the character editor — or use “Save to a character” above if the page already exists.
